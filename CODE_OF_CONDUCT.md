# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The full text at that link applies to everyone who takes part in the project's spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews. In short:

- Be respectful and considerate. Assume good intent, and criticize ideas and code, not people.
- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, sexualized language or attention, and publishing someone's private information are not acceptable.
- Accept that people have different experience and different viewpoints, and that a maintainer may decline a contribution.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior in the project's spaces breaks these rules, tell the maintainer privately, through the contact options on the maintainer's GitHub profile (`https://github.com/RyoHirota68`). Do not open a public issue for it. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may remove or edit comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break these rules, and may block a person from the project temporarily or permanently.
